## Description

Richard Sutton — father of reinforcement learning, 2024 Turing Award winner, author of The Bitter Lesson — argues that LLMs are a dead end and that real intelligence requires continual experience-based learning, not human-imitation. A foundational interview for the LLM-vs-RL debate.

## Episode outline

- The Bitter Lesson, revisited
- Why LLMs are a dead end
- What 'real' learning looks like
- Continual learning and embodied agents
