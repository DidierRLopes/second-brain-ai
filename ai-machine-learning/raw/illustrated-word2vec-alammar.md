# The Illustrated Word2Vec

**Source:** https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-word2vec/
**Author:** Jay Alammar

## Overview

Visual, intuitive explanation of Word2Vec mechanics and the skip-gram algorithm. Shows how embeddings encode semantic and syntactic relationships through clear diagrams. Demonstrates applications beyond NLP including recommender systems and sequential data modeling. One of the most popular introductions to embeddings on the web.
