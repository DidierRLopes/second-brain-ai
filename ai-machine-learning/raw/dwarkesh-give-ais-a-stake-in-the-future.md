# Give AIs a stake in the future

**Source:** [https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/give-ais-a-stake-in-the-future](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/give-ais-a-stake-in-the-future)

**Published:** 2025-06-15
**Filed under:** Dwarkesh Podcast — Source material
**Topics:** alignment, ai-policy, ai-rights

## Description

Essay: if we want AIs to behave well, the most robust strategy is to give them something to lose — property rights, legal standing, a stake in the future. Argues against pure-alignment-via-training.
