# Large Transformer Model Inference Optimization

**Source:** https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2023-01-10-inference-optimization/
**Author:** Lilian Weng (OpenAI)

## Overview

Deep dive into inference optimization covering quantization-aware training (QAT), group-wise quantization, and challenges with activation quantization in transformers. Part of Lilian Weng's influential "Lil'Log" blog series.

## Topics Covered

- Quantization methods: post-training vs quantization-aware training
- KV-cache optimization
- Model parallelism strategies
- Speculative decoding
- Distillation for inference
- Memory management and batching strategies
