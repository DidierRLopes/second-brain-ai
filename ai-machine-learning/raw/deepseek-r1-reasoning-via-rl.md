# DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948
**Authors:** DeepSeek-AI (200+ authors)
**Published:** January 2025

## Key Concepts

Demonstrates that sophisticated reasoning abilities (verification, reflection, exploring alternatives) can emerge purely through reinforcement learning without human-annotated reasoning demonstrations. Uses Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) on DeepSeek-V3 Base.

## Why It Matters

- Paradigm shift: reasoning emerges from RL alone, no reasoning demonstrations needed
- Models naturally develop self-verification and backtracking behaviors
- Open-source alternative to OpenAI's o1/o3 reasoning approach
- Competitive with o1 on math, code, and science benchmarks
- Distilled versions (1.5B to 70B) retain strong reasoning capabilities
- Showed that reasoning is a learnable behavior, not just a prompting trick
