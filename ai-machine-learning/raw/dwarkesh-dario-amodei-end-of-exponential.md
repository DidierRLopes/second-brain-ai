## Description

Dario Amodei thinks we are just a few years away from AGI — 'a country of geniuses in a data center'. They discuss the scaling hypothesis in the current RL regime, why task-specific RL might lead to generalization, and how AI will diffuse throughout the economy. Plus Anthropic's revenue projections, compute commitments, and path to profitability.

## Episode outline

- What exactly are we scaling?
- Is diffusion cope?
- Is continual learning necessary?
- If AGI is imminent, why not buy more compute?
- How will AI labs actually make profit?
- Will regulations destroy the boons of AGI?
- Why can't China and America both have a country of geniuses in a datacenter?
