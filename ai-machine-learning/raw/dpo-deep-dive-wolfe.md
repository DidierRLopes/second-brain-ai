# Direct Preference Optimization — Deep (Learning) Focus

**Source:** https://cameronrwolfe.substack.com/p/direct-preference-optimization
**Author:** Cameron R. Wolfe, Ph.D. (Netflix)

## Overview

In-depth analysis of the DPO algorithm, comparing it with RLHF and explaining the simplified reward modeling approach. Walks through the mathematical derivation and practical benefits of DPO over PPO-based alignment.

## Topics Covered

- RLHF pipeline breakdown and its complexity
- Mathematical derivation of the DPO objective
- Why DPO is equivalent to implicit reward modeling
- Practical comparison: DPO vs PPO stability and performance
- Variants and extensions of DPO
