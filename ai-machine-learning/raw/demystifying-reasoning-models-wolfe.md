# Demystifying Reasoning Models — Deep (Learning) Focus

**Source:** https://cameronrwolfe.substack.com/p/demystifying-reasoning-models
**Author:** Cameron R. Wolfe, Ph.D. (Netflix)

## Overview

In-depth analysis of reasoning models (o1, o3, DeepSeek-R1, etc.), explaining how test-time compute scaling works, the role of RL in training reasoning capabilities, and how these models differ from standard LLMs.

## Topics Covered

- Test-time compute scaling: thinking longer = better answers
- How RL trains reasoning without human demonstrations
- Comparison of reasoning approaches across providers
- Chain-of-thought vs learned reasoning
- Implications for AI capability scaling
