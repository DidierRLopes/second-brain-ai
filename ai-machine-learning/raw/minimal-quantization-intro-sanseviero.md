# A Minimal Introduction to Quantization

**Source:** https://osanseviero.github.io/hackerllama/blog/posts/minimal-quantize-intro/
**Author:** Omar Sanseviero (Google DeepMind)

## Overview

Accessible introduction to quantization techniques including QLoRA, with practical examples showing how to fine-tune 7B parameter models on free Google Colab instances. Bridges theory and practice for developers getting started with quantization.

## Topics Covered

- What quantization is and why it matters
- Different quantization approaches (PTQ vs QAT)
- Practical QLoRA walkthrough
- Hardware requirements and tradeoffs
